Need a hand?

Find an answer, check your microphone and speaker, or contact us. Most sound problems take just a few seconds to fix.

Common questions
Why can't my phone hear the sound?

Try these quick fixes:

  • Turn up the volume on the sending device.
  • Keep the devices 10–30 cm apart.
  • Point the speaker towards the other device's microphone.
  • On the receiving device, tap Listen for sound before sending.
  • Make sure nothing is covering either speaker or microphone.
  • Allow microphone access in your browser settings, then reload the page.
Can I change the notification sound?

Yes. Open Catch and tap the sound option below the listening circle.

Choose a chime, ding, or whoosh. You can also use your own sound.

This sound is only played on your device. It isn't uploaded or sent with your message.

How do I open a .spim file without internet?

Open Catch, select the file option, and choose your .spim file.

Your message opens directly in your browser. No internet connection is needed.

Keep the file safe, especially if it contains a private message.

Are my messages stored online?

It depends on how you send them.

  • Link or QR code: Your message is stored online so the recipient can open it.
  • Private message: Your message is encrypted before it leaves your browser.
  • SPIM file: Your message can be opened without connecting to a server.
  • Sound: Your message is sent directly between devices.
What is a one-time message?

A one-time message can only be opened once.

After the recipient opens it, the message is deleted and its link stops working. Even the sender cannot open it again.

Test your devices

Check that your microphone and speaker are working before sending.

Microphone Not tested

Speak normally for a few seconds.

Speaker Not tested

Play a test sound to check your volume.

Turn up your volume. You should hear a short tone.

Nothing is recorded. Your microphone switches off when you finish testing.
