Most problems are a muted mic or a covered speaker. Test both here in seconds, then read the answer you need.

Common questions
Why didn't my phone hear the sound?

Sound is physical, so check the room before the software:

  • Turn the sending device's volume up — media volume, not just the ringer.
  • Hold the devices 10–30 cm apart, speaker toward microphone.
  • Tap Listen for sound on the receiving device before you press Create & beam.
  • Uncover both the speaker and the mic. Cases and hands are enough to block the sound.
  • If the mic was blocked in the browser, allow it in the address-bar permissions and reload.
How do I change the drop sound?

Open the Catch tab and use the small sound pill under the listen orb. Pick chime, ding, or whoosh — or upload your own clip with the speaker icon.

The sound plays on your device when a capsule lands. It is never uploaded, and it never travels with the capsule.

How do I open a .spim file offline?

Go to Catch and drop the file on the file area, or press it and choose the file. It opens in the tab with no network call at all.

A .spim carries the whole capsule. If it was created as private, the key is inside the file, so keep the file itself somewhere safe.

Is my data stored on your servers?

Only if you choose a link or QR. That capsule is stored so the recipient can open it, and single-view capsules are destroyed after the first open.

Private capsules are encrypted in your browser; the key lives in the link after the # and is never sent to us. A .spim file or a sound beam never touches a server.

What happens to a single-view capsule?

It can be opened once. After the recipient opens it, the capsule is consumed and the link stops working — including for the sender.
