Why SPIM

Your words should
arrive intact.

A link is a promise someone else keeps. A capsule is one you can check yourself — byte for byte, with or without a network.

01

Own your memory

A capsule is self-contained. The words, the structure, the seal — all of it lives with the thing you sent, not in a database you rent. Export it, keep it, open it in ten years.

02

Zero-network freedom

Two devices in a room need no Wi-Fi, no server, no account. A sound carries the link. A .spim file carries everything else. Both work when the internet does not.

03

Cryptographic truth

Every capsule is sealed with BLAKE3. If a single byte changes — in transit, on disk, or by an editor — the seal breaks and the page says so. Trust the maths, not the messenger.
