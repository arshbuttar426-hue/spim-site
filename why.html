Why SPIM

Your words should arrive exactly as you sent them.

A link depends on someone else's service. SPIM lets you send messages that you can check yourself — even without internet.

01

Keep your messages

Your message stays together with everything needed to open it. Save it, share it, or open it years later. You don't need to rely on a database or subscription.

02

Works without internet

Send between two devices without Wi-Fi, a server, or an account. Use sound to send the message and a .spim file to carry it. It works even when the internet doesn't.

03

Know if anything changed

Every message has a built-in security check. If someone changes the message, SPIM detects it and tells you. You don't have to take anyone's word for it.
