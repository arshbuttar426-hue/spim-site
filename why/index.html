WHY IT MATTERS

Why this exists

Information is scattered. A meeting note in Slack, an API endpoint in Notion, a decision in an email, a link in Discord — and when someone needs one piece, they scroll through five places.

Problem
Five separate links, one long doc no one reads, repeated “where is…?”
Solution
Put several pieces in one place. Send one link. Let them find what they need.

How it works — without the jargon

You paste notes. We keep your source exactly as you typed it, and we build a searchable view that is reconstructed without AI. Your recipient opens one link and searches across everything — instantly, on their phone, without an account.
